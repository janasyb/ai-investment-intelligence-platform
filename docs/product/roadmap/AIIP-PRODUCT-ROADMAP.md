# AIIP PRODUCT ROADMAP

**Company:** AIIP Technologies  
**Product:** AI Investment Intelligence Platform  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Authoritative  
**Last Updated:** 2026-09-09

---

# 1. PURPOSE

This roadmap defines the controlled sequence in which AIIP Technologies develops its product and business capabilities.

The roadmap exists to prevent:

- random feature development
- premature engineering
- scope creep
- technology-driven product decisions
- building features without customer evidence
- confusing "possible" with "necessary"

The roadmap is evidence-driven and may change when new evidence justifies a change.

---

# 2. ROADMAP PRINCIPLE

AIIP does not build everything it can build.

AIIP builds the highest-value validated capability that advances the company's current strategic objective.

The current strategic objective is:

> Validate that AIIP can create sufficiently valuable investment decision intelligence for real digital-asset investors to pay for it.

# STAGE 1 — COMMERCIAL VALIDATION

### Objective

Determine whether real digital-asset investors have a sufficiently important problem that AIIP can solve and monetize.

### Primary questions

1. Who is the strongest initial customer?
2. What investment decision problem is most painful?
3. What intelligence do they currently lack?
4. What are they currently using?
5. What does the problem cost them?
6. Will they pay for a better solution?
7. Will they use the solution repeatedly?

### Priority initiatives

#### AIIP-011 — Commercial Validation Framework

Status: COMPLETE

#### Customer Discovery

Status: ACTIVE

#### Investment Decision Report

Status: ACTIVE

The Investment Decision Report is the primary early commercial-validation artifact.

### Success condition

At least one real customer pays for an AIIP intelligence product.

Stronger validation includes:

- repeated purchases
- referrals
- recurring demand
- willingness to subscribe
- willingness to pay more
- measurable decision-process improvement

# 4. PRIORITY RULE

When selecting the next initiative, prioritize according to:

1. Customer evidence
2. Commercial value
3. Strategic alignment
4. Risk reduction
5. Dependency
6. Technical feasibility
7. Engineering convenience

---

# 5. ROADMAP STATUS DEFINITIONS

### PROPOSED

Idea under consideration.

No implementation authorized.

### VALIDATING

Evidence is being gathered.

Implementation should remain minimal.

### APPROVED

The initiative has passed product review and may enter implementation.

### ACTIVE

Implementation is underway.

### BLOCKED

Implementation cannot proceed because a dependency or decision is unresolved.

### COMPLETE

Acceptance criteria have been satisfied and the initiative has been released/merged.

### DEFERRED

The initiative remains valid but is intentionally postponed.

### REJECTED

Evidence or strategy does not justify implementation.

---

# 6. ROADMAP CHANGE CONTROL

A roadmap item may be added, removed, reordered, or redefined only when supported by:

- customer evidence
- commercial evidence
- technical evidence
- security requirements
- legal/regulatory requirements
- strategic decision

The reason for a material change must be recorded in the Decision Register.
