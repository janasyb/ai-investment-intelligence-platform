# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

AIIP-018 establishes the minimum commercial-validation workflow required to determine whether AIIP can create sufficiently valuable investment decision intelligence for a real digital-asset decision maker to pay for it.

AIIP already has:

- customer-discovery foundations
- early-access request collection
- internal early-access operations
- a defined Decision Intelligence Report concept
- a research framework covering multiple dimensions of digital-asset analysis

AIIP-018 converts that concept into a controlled manual commercial-validation workflow.

The objective is not to build an automated research platform.

The objective is to validate the commercial proposition using real customer behavior.

The initiative must answer:

> Will a real digital-asset decision maker with a current investment decision provide sufficient information for AIIP to produce a useful Decision Intelligence Report, use the report, and make an actual payment for it?

---

# 2. STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE

Validate an initial paid AIIP intelligence workflow and determine whether the evidence justifies continued commercial validation and eventual repeatable productization.

The initiative should establish evidence for the following progression:

`Real Signal`
-> `Qualified Decision Maker`
-> `Real Investment Decision`
-> `Customer Input`
-> `Research`
-> `Decision Intelligence Report`
-> `Customer Delivery`
-> `Customer Use`
-> `Payment`
-> `Post-Delivery Evidence`

The initiative is successful only when evidence progresses beyond statements such as:

- "this sounds useful"
- "I would probably use this"
- "AI could help"

Behavioral evidence is required.

---

# 3. BUSINESS PROBLEM

Digital-asset investors can access large quantities of market information, research, commentary, and data.

The commercial question for AIIP is not whether information exists.

The commercial question is whether AIIP can reduce a meaningful decision-making problem by organizing relevant evidence into a structured decision-intelligence artifact.

Without customer validation, AIIP risks building:

- an attractive research interface without a paying use case
- generic AI analysis without differentiated value
- excessive automation before the workflow is understood
- features that customers say they like but do not pay for

AIIP-018 therefore prioritizes real customer behavior over feature volume.

---

# 4. PRIMARY USER

The primary participant is:

> A real digital-asset decision maker who is actively evaluating a current investment decision.

The participant should have a concrete decision such as:

- BUY
- HOLD
- SELL
- WAIT

The decision must be current or sufficiently recent to support a realistic research workflow.

The initiative is not intended to validate hypothetical users who are merely interested in digital assets.

---

# 5. CORE HYPOTHESIS

If AIIP can synthesize relevant digital-asset evidence into a structured Decision Intelligence Report for a real decision maker, then at least some decision makers will perceive sufficient value to:

1. provide decision context
2. review the resulting report
3. use the report in their decision process
4. provide specific feedback
5. pay for the report

The hypothesis is not considered validated by positive verbal feedback alone.

---

# 6. COMMERCIAL VALIDATION QUESTIONS

AIIP-018 must produce evidence for these questions:

### Customer

Who is most likely to use and pay for the report?

### Problem

What specific investment-decision problem caused the customer to seek help?

### Existing workflow

What sources, tools, analysts, communities, spreadsheets, dashboards, or research processes does the customer currently use?

### Friction

What part of the existing workflow is expensive, slow, confusing, fragmented, or difficult?

### Value

What did the customer find materially useful in the AIIP report?

### Behavior

Did the customer actually use the report?

### Payment

Did the customer make an actual payment rather than merely express willingness to pay?

### Repeatability

Would the same customer require another report for a future decision?

# 7. IN SCOPE

## 7.1 Customer Qualification

Identify a prospect who:

- actively invests or makes digital-asset investment decisions
- has a current decision to evaluate
- can describe the decision context
- is willing to participate in the research workflow
- can review a delivered report

The participant should have a decision sufficiently close to action that the
research could reasonably affect the participant's current research process.

Purely hypothetical questions or requests for general market commentary do not
qualify as the primary validation case.

---

## 7.2 Customer Input Collection

Collect only information necessary to produce the report.

Potential fields:

- customer identifier
- asset under consideration
- decision type
- decision timeframe
- customer's stated thesis
- customer's main concerns
- customer's research questions
- evidence or sources already considered
- relevant alternatives being considered
- desired report focus
- report delivery details

The workflow must not request:

- passwords
- private keys
- seed phrases
- exchange credentials
- wallet credentials
- API secrets
- authentication codes
- unnecessary personally sensitive information

---

## 7.3 Decision Intelligence Report

The initial report should remain human-reviewed and manually produced.

The report should synthesize relevant evidence across applicable dimensions, including:

- market intelligence
- fundamental/product analysis
- tokenomics
- liquidity and execution considerations
- risk intelligence
- evidence synthesis

The exact dimensions may vary by asset and decision.

The report must distinguish:

- observed facts
- sourced claims
- analytical interpretation
- uncertainty
- conflicting evidence
- research limitations

---

## 7.4 Report Delivery

The report should be delivered directly to the customer through an appropriate controlled channel.

The initial workflow does not require:

- customer account infrastructure
- automated report generation
- automated report delivery infrastructure
- subscription billing
- public report dashboards

---

## 7.5 Customer Evaluation

After delivery, collect structured evidence concerning:

- whether the report was reviewed
- which sections were useful
- which sections were unclear
- which evidence was new
- which evidence conflicted with the customer's existing view
- whether the report changed, confirmed, or clarified the customer's decision process
- whether the report saved time or reduced research friction
- what was missing

---

## 7.6 Payment Validation

Commercial validation must test actual payment behavior.

A payment-validation event requires:

1. a defined report scope
2. an agreed price
3. customer acceptance of the offer
4. actual transfer of money
5. report delivery
6. confirmation that the customer received the deliverable

The initial proposed commercial-validation price is:

> **USD 49 per Decision Intelligence Report**

This price is a validation hypothesis, not an established permanent product price.

The initiative must not build a payment-processing platform merely to test willingness to pay.

The validation payment must be an actual completed commercial transaction, not a verbal commitment, stated willingness to pay, or unpaid invoice.

For validation reporting, record:
- price offered
- price accepted
- payment date
- payment method at a non-sensitive level
- whether the payment was later refunded
- report-delivery date

# 9. REPORT DEFINITION

The initial Decision Intelligence Report should be a structured research artifact rather than a generic AI-generated answer.

## Minimum report structure

### 9.1 Decision Context

Document:

- asset
- decision under consideration
- customer's stated question
- relevant timeframe
- key assumptions
- research date/time
- information cutoff date/time

### 9.2 Executive Research Summary

Summarize the most decision-relevant findings.

The section must distinguish evidence from interpretation.

### 9.3 Market Context

Relevant market conditions and drivers applicable to the decision.

### 9.4 Fundamental / Project Analysis

Relevant characteristics of the asset, protocol, project, product, adoption, development activity, or economic model.

### 9.5 Tokenomics

Where applicable:

- supply structure
- issuance
- unlocks
- concentration
- incentives
- distribution
- demand relationships

### 9.6 Liquidity and Execution

Where applicable:

- liquidity conditions
- trading venues
- spread considerations
- market-depth considerations
- execution constraints

### 9.7 Risk Intelligence

Relevant risks, including where applicable:

- market risk
- liquidity risk
- concentration risk
- tokenomic risk
- technological risk
- protocol risk
- regulatory uncertainty
- counterparty or venue risk
- information uncertainty

### 9.8 Evidence Matrix

Identify:

- supporting evidence
- contradictory evidence
- unresolved questions
- evidence quality
- source references

### 9.9 Decision Considerations

Present factors the customer should consider.

Do not present guaranteed outcomes.

Do not represent speculative scenarios as facts.

### 9.10 Research Limitations

Document:

- missing information
- stale information
- uncertain claims
- source limitations
- analytical assumptions

### 9.11 Sources

Provide traceable source references for material research claims.

# 10. REPORT PRINCIPLES

The Decision Intelligence Report must be:

- evidence-first
- source-aware
- structured
- transparent about uncertainty
- reproducible where practical
- human-reviewed
- understandable to the intended customer
- bounded to the customer's stated decision

The report must not:

- guarantee returns
- imply certainty where evidence is uncertain
- fabricate sources
- conceal contradictory evidence
- claim access to private data that AIIP does not possess
- misrepresent AI-generated analysis as verified fact
- request or store private keys, passwords, or exchange credentials

The product is intended to support structured research and decision analysis, not guarantee investment outcomes.

The report must identify the research timeframe so that time-sensitive information is not presented as permanently current.

# 11. VALIDATION WORKFLOW

AIIP-018 should follow this sequence:

## Step 1 - Identify

Find a real candidate with a current digital-asset decision.

## Step 2 - Qualify

Confirm that the candidate has a real decision and a meaningful research need.

## Step 3 - Interview

Understand:

- what decision they are making
- why the decision matters
- what they already researched
- where their existing process fails
- what information they still need

## Step 4 - Scope

Define the report's:

- asset
- decision question
- timeframe
- research dimensions
- delivery expectation
- commercial price

## Step 5 - Commercial Offer

Present the defined report scope, price, deliverable, and expected delivery timeframe.

Record whether the customer:
- accepts
- declines
- requests changes
- asks for more information
- pays

The USD 49 price is the initial experiment price only. AIIP must not treat
successful payment at this price as evidence that USD 49 is the optimal,
final, or scalable product price.

## Step 6 - Payment Confirmation

Record the actual payment event before beginning paid report production, unless a specific validation experiment intentionally tests another commercial model.

## Step 7 - Research

Produce the report using the defined research framework.

## Step 8 - Human Review

Check:

- factual accuracy
- source traceability
- contradictory evidence
- unsupported claims
- missing material risks
- report completeness

## Step 9 - Deliver

Provide the report to the customer.

## Step 10 - Observe

Determine whether the customer actually reads or uses the report.

## Step 11 - Interview

Collect post-delivery evidence.

## Step 12 - Record

Update customer-discovery records with the evidence.

# 12. EVIDENCE STANDARD

Customer evidence should be classified.

## Weak Evidence

Examples:

- "Sounds useful."
- "I might pay for this."
- "AI could save me time."

Weak evidence does not validate the commercial hypothesis.

## Moderate Evidence

Examples:

- recurring problem
- repeated research effort
- multiple tools currently used
- meaningful time spent researching
- existing spending on related information products

Moderate evidence supports continued validation.

## Strong Evidence

Examples:

- specific real decision
- specific measurable research problem
- customer provides required context
- customer completes a report test
- customer uses the delivered report
- customer changes or confirms part of their workflow based on the report
- customer pays actual money
- customer requests another report

Strong evidence is required for commercialization decisions.

AIIP-018 produces meaningful commercial-validation evidence when the initiative
completes the defined workflow and records the resulting customer behavior.

The core monetization hypothesis is supported only when the validation produces
an actual completed commercial transaction.

The absence of payment is itself valid commercial evidence and must be recorded
rather than treated as an implementation failure.

### SC-01

At least one real digital-asset decision maker completes the defined validation workflow.

### SC-02

The decision is based on a real current or recent investment decision.

### SC-03

AIIP receives sufficient customer input to produce the report without requesting prohibited sensitive credentials.

### SC-04

AIIP produces and human-reviews a complete Decision Intelligence Report.

### SC-05

The customer receives the report.

### SC-06

Post-delivery customer evidence is captured.

### SC-07

The payment hypothesis is tested through an actual commercial transaction.

### SC-08

The validation record distinguishes verbal interest from behavioral evidence.

### SC-09

At least one concrete improvement to the report workflow is identified from customer feedback, or the evidence shows that the current workflow is insufficient.

### SC-10

The evidence supports a documented decision about the next product-validation step.

# 16. RISKS

## Risk 1 - Building Before Validation

AIIP could automate a workflow that customers do not value.

### Mitigation

Keep report production manual and human-reviewed.

---

## Risk 2 - Mistaking Interest for Demand

Prospects may express enthusiasm but not pay.

### Mitigation

Require actual behavioral and payment evidence.

---

## Risk 3 - Report Quality Variability

Manual research may vary by operator or asset.

### Mitigation

Use a defined report template, research checklist, source requirements, and human review.

---

## Risk 4 - Unsupported Analysis

AI-generated content could contain unsupported or inaccurate claims.

### Mitigation

Require source traceability, evidence review, uncertainty disclosure, and human validation.

---

## Risk 5 - Sensitive Customer Data

Customer-provided information could exceed what is required.

### Mitigation

Collect the minimum necessary information and explicitly prohibit credentials and secrets.

---

## Risk 6 - Commercial Process Overengineering

AIIP could build payment, CRM, account, or automation systems before demand is proven.

### Mitigation

Use manual operational processes for validation.

# 17. SECURITY AND PRIVACY REQUIREMENTS

AIIP-018 must:

- minimize customer data collection
- prohibit collection of passwords and private keys
- prohibit collection of seed phrases
- prohibit collection of exchange credentials
- prohibit collection of API secrets
- avoid storing unnecessary financial-account details
- protect customer research information from public exposure
- distinguish customer-provided information from sourced market information
- preserve source traceability
- prevent secrets from entering repository files or logs

No new customer-authentication system is authorized by AIIP-018.

---

# 18. LEGAL / COMPLIANCE BOUNDARY

AIIP-018 must not make unsupported claims about:

- guaranteed returns
- guaranteed investment outcomes
- certainty of predictions
- regulatory status
- licensing status
- legal suitability

The report should clearly describe its research purpose and limitations.

Any jurisdiction-specific regulatory requirements discovered during validation must be recorded as a decision or blocker rather than silently bypassed.

AIIP-018 does not establish that the Decision Intelligence Report is legally classified as research, education, or investment advice in any particular jurisdiction.

Before commercializing the workflow in a jurisdiction where legal or regulatory requirements may apply, AIIP must identify the applicable requirements and obtain appropriate professional advice where necessary.

No statement in this PRD authorizes AIIP to provide regulated financial services.

# 24. COMMERCIAL DECISION GATE

Following the validation cycle, AIIP must document one of the following evidence-based next actions:

### Continue Validation

Evidence supports further testing of the same customer/problem/report hypothesis.

### Revise Hypothesis

Customer evidence indicates that the customer segment, problem, report structure, pricing hypothesis, or workflow requires material revision.

### Expand Validation

Evidence supports testing the workflow with additional qualified customers.

### Defer

Evidence is insufficient or operational/legal constraints prevent continued testing.

### Stop

Evidence does not justify continued investment in the tested hypothesis.

The decision must be based on recorded customer behavior and evidence rather than implementation effort already incurred.
