# 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

AIIP is an AI-powered investment intelligence platform focused initially on digital assets.

The platform is designed to transform fragmented investment information into structured investment decision intelligence.

AIIP helps investors:

Discover → Analyze → Synthesize → Decide → Learn

The product is intended to become a global investment-intelligence company rather than a collection of disconnected financial tools.

---

# 2. VISION

Build the world's leading AI-powered investment intelligence platform for digital assets.

The long-term objective is to create a trusted intelligence layer between:

Data
→ Information
→ Research
→ Intelligence
→ Decision

---

# 3. MISSION

Help investors make more informed, structured, and evidence-aware investment decisions by transforming fragmented digital-asset information into usable investment intelligence.

---

# 4. PRODUCT PROMISE

AIIP helps users:

- find relevant information
- understand important factors
- analyze evidence
- identify risks
- compare conflicting signals
- synthesize research
- structure investment decisions

AIIP does not promise:

- profits
- guaranteed returns
- certain predictions
- risk-free investments
- perfect information

---

# 5. CORE CUSTOMER PROBLEM

Digital-asset investors face significant information fragmentation.

Relevant information may exist across:

- market data
- news
- research
- social signals
- tokenomics
- on-chain data
- liquidity data
- project documentation
- community information
- macroeconomic information

The central problem is therefore not merely access to information.

It is:

> determining what matters, evaluating conflicting evidence, and translating research into a disciplined decision process.

---

# 6. TARGET CUSTOMER

The initial target customer is an individual who actively makes digital-asset investment decisions.

Relevant behaviors include:

- BUY decisions

- portfolio allocation
- investment research
- information synthesis
- due diligence

The strongest early signal is:

# 8. JOB TO BE DONE

When considering a digital-asset investment decision, the customer needs to:

1. Understand the asset.
2. Understand market conditions.
3. Evaluate fundamentals.
4. Understand tokenomics.
5. Assess liquidity and execution.
6. Identify risks.
7. Resolve conflicting evidence.
8. Determine what evidence matters.
9. Form a structured decision.

AIIP should reduce the time and cognitive effort required to perform this process.

---

# 33. FEATURE APPROVAL

A meaningful feature enters development only when:

1. The problem is defined.
2. The customer is defined.
3. The reason for solving it now is understood.
4. Evidence supports the problem.
5. The feature fits product strategy.
6. Scope is defined.
7. Acceptance criteria exist.
8. Out-of-scope items are defined.

---

# 34. CHANGE MANAGEMENT

If new evidence contradicts the PRD:

1. Document the evidence.
2. Identify the conflict.
3. Evaluate the strategic consequence.
4. Update the relevant requirement.
5. Record the decision.
6. Change implementation.

The PRD is authoritative but not immutable.

Evidence can change strategy.

---

# 35. OUT-OF-ROADMAP RULE

When a new idea appears:

> "Let's build X."

The required questions are:

1. What customer problem does X solve?
2. Who has that problem?
3. What evidence demonstrates it?
4. How important is it?
5. How does it fit the product strategy?
6. Why now?
7. What is the smallest test?
8. What existing roadmap item should move if X becomes a priority?

---

# 36. PRODUCT COMPLETION

A feature is not complete merely because:

- code exists
- the UI looks good
- an API responds
- tests pass

A feature is complete when:

- requirements are satisfied
- acceptance criteria pass
- security is acceptable
- automated tests pass
- CI passes
- documentation is updated
- operational requirements are satisfied
- product behavior is understood

---

# 37. MASTER ROADMAP CONTROL

Every AIIP development task must identify:

> The approved roadmap objective it advances.

If it does not advance an approved objective, it becomes a proposal.

It does not automatically become development work.

---

Customer uncertainty

The company should continuously ask:

> What uncertainty are we removing next?
