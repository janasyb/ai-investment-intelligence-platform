# AIIP Decision Intelligence Report - Research Review Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist is mandatory for the AIIP-018 manual Decision Intelligence Report workflow.

The reviewer confirms that the report is suitable for delivery only after the required checks are complete.

---

## 1. Scope

- [ ] The asset matches the agreed report scope.
- [ ] The decision type is correctly recorded.
- [ ] The customer's actual decision question is represented accurately.
- [ ] The research timeframe is recorded.
- [ ] The information cutoff date/time is recorded.
- [ ] The report remains bounded to the customer's stated decision.

---

## 2. Customer Context

- [ ] Customer-provided information is clearly distinguishable from externally sourced information.
- [ ] The customer's stated thesis is represented accurately.
- [ ] Material customer concerns are addressed.
- [ ] The report does not invent customer circumstances.
- [ ] No unnecessary sensitive personal information is included.

---

## 3. Evidence Quality

- [ ] Material claims have traceable sources.
- [ ] Sources are appropriate to the claim being made.
- [ ] Dates are checked for time-sensitive information.
- [ ] Important conflicting evidence is included.
- [ ] Evidence quality is identified where material.
- [ ] Unresolved questions are disclosed.

---

## 4. Analytical Integrity

- [ ] Observed facts are distinguished from interpretation.
- [ ] Analytical conclusions are supported by presented evidence.
- [ ] Speculative scenarios are clearly identified.
- [ ] Unsupported claims have been removed or corrected.
- [ ] Numerical calculations, percentages, and quantities have been checked.
- [ ] The report does not imply certainty where evidence is uncertain.

---

## 5. Market / Asset Analysis

- [ ] Market conditions relevant to the decision are covered.
- [ ] Fundamental / project considerations are covered where applicable.
- [ ] On-chain evidence is included where applicable.
- [ ] Tokenomics are reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] Liquidity and market-structure considerations are reviewed.
- [ ] Competitive or alternative considerations are reviewed where applicable.

---

## 6. Risk Review

- [ ] Material market risks are considered.
- [ ] Liquidity risks are considered.
- [ ] Concentration risks are considered where applicable.
- [ ] Tokenomic risks are considered where applicable.
- [ ] Technology / protocol risks are considered where applicable.
- [ ] Regulatory uncertainty is disclosed where relevant.
- [ ] Counterparty / venue risks are considered where relevant.
- [ ] Information uncertainty is disclosed.

---

## 7. Decision Considerations

- [ ] Decision considerations are tied to the customer's stated question.
- [ ] Contradictory evidence is not concealed.
- [ ] No guaranteed outcome is presented.
- [ ] No guaranteed return is presented.
- [ ] The report does not claim certainty of prediction.
- [ ] The customer remains responsible for the investment decision.

---

## 8. Privacy / Security

- [ ] No passwords are included.
- [ ] No private keys are included.
- [ ] No seed phrases are included.
- [ ] No exchange credentials are included.
- [ ] No wallet credentials are included.
- [ ] No API secrets are included.
- [ ] No authentication codes are included.
- [ ] No unnecessary financial-account information is included.
- [ ] No secrets have been written to repository files or logs.

---

## 9. Final Delivery Gate

- [ ] Report is complete.
- [ ] Sources are traceable.
- [ ] Research limitations are disclosed.
- [ ] Disclaimer is present.
- [ ] Human review is complete.
- [ ] Required revisions are resolved.
- [ ] Correct report version is prepared for delivery.

# Reviewer Rule

Do not approve the report merely because it is readable.

Approve only when the report is sufficiently supported, transparent about uncertainty, complete within scope, and suitable for the defined commercial-validation experiment.
