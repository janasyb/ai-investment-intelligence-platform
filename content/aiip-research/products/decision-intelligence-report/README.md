# AIIP Digital Asset Decision Intelligence Report

## Overview

The AIIP Digital Asset Decision Intelligence Report is the initial concierge product being used to validate the AIIP customer and product hypotheses.

It is designed for an investor who is actively evaluating a specific digital-asset decision.

## Supported Decision Types

- BUY
- HOLD
- SELL
- WAIT

## Product Objective

Transform relevant digital-asset information into a structured research framework that helps the customer understand:

- What is happening
- Why it may be happening
- What supports the investment thesis
- What contradicts the investment thesis
- What risks exist
- What could invalidate the thesis
- What should be monitored

## V0 Delivery

The first version is intentionally concierge-based.
