# AIIP Digital Asset Decision Intelligence Report

## Product Status

V0 — CUSTOMER VALIDATION

## Purpose

Provide structured intelligence to help a customer evaluate a specific digital-asset investment decision.

The product is designed to organize and analyze relevant evidence rather than guarantee a future market outcome.

---

# Target Customer

An active digital-asset investor or trader who is currently making a meaningful decision about an asset.

Typical decisions:

- BUY
- HOLD
- SELL
- WAIT

---

# Customer Input

The customer provides:

- Asset
- Current decision
- Time horizon
- Primary concern
- Optional context about their situation

---

# Report Output

## 1. Executive Summary

Concise overview of the evidence.

## 2. Market Conditions

Relevant market environment.

## 3. Fundamental Analysis

Assessment of the underlying asset/project.

## 4. On-Chain Analysis

Relevant blockchain/network activity where applicable.

## 5. Tokenomics

Supply, distribution, emissions, unlocks, incentives, and other relevant factors.

## 6. Liquidity & Market Structure

Liquidity, trading activity, market depth, volatility, and relevant market-structure considerations.

## 7. Competitive Position

Relevant competitors, alternatives, and differentiation.

## 8. Bull Case

Evidence supporting the positive thesis.

## 9. Bear Case

Evidence contradicting or challenging the positive thesis.

## 10. Key Risks

Material risks identified from available evidence.

## 11. Thesis Invalidation

Conditions that could invalidate the investment thesis.

## 12. What Has Changed?

Important recent developments.

## 13. What To Monitor

Key indicators or events to watch.

## 14. Decision Framework

BUY / HOLD / SELL / WAIT assessment based on the defined methodology.

## 15. Confidence & Limitations

Explain:

- evidence quality
- uncertainty
- missing information
- analytical limitations

---

# Product Principle

AIIP does not promise investment returns.

AIIP provides research and decision-support intelligence.

The customer remains responsible for their investment decisions.

---

# V0 Delivery Model

Initially:

MANUAL / CONCIERGE

Customer submits request.

AIIP produces the report.

Customer provides feedback.

Repeated requirements are identified.

Validated requirements become automated product capabilities.
