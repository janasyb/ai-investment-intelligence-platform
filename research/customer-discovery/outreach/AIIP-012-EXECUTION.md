# AIIP-012 Customer Outreach Execution

## Objective

Convert qualified customer signals into genuine customer conversations and structured interviews.

AIIP-012 is an execution phase.

The objective is not to maximize outreach volume.

The objective is to determine whether people experiencing the problems identified in customer discovery will:

1. Respond.
2. Discuss their current workflow.
3. Participate in an interview.
4. Test an AIIP product.
5. Consider paying for it.

## Current Validation Targets

AIIP-012 should aim to establish:

- 20+ qualified customer signals
- 10+ genuine customer conversations
- 5+ recurring problem confirmations
- 3+ product tests
- 1+ paying customer

These are validation targets.

They must not be manufactured through artificial outreach volume or optimistic classification.

---

## Priority Order

### Priority 1 — Confirmed Digital-Asset Decision Intent

Highest priority should be given to people who publicly demonstrate:

- Active crypto investment activity.
- BUY decisions.
- HOLD decisions.
- SELL decisions.
- WAIT decisions.
- Asset selection problems.
- Risk evaluation.
- Exchange evaluation.
- Liquidity concerns.
- Investment research problems.
- Portfolio allocation problems.

### Priority 2 — Strong Investment Intent With Unknown Digital-Asset Exposure

Examples:

- Investors deploying substantial capital.
- Investors deciding where to allocate newly available capital.
- Investors actively researching investment opportunities.

These leads require qualification before being treated as AIIP core customers.

### Priority 3 — Adjacent Problems

Examples:

- Crypto-to-fiat conversion.
- Exchange operational issues.
- General trading education.
- General financial questions.

These may reveal useful customer problems but should not automatically influence the AIIP V1 product thesis.

# Outreach Rules

## Rule 1 — Respond to the Actual Problem

Every outreach message must reference something the person actually said.

Do not send generic messages such as:

> Hi, I'm building an AI investment platform. Would you like to try it?

Instead, respond to the specific problem described.

---

## Rule 2 — Do Not Pretend

Never:

- Pretend to be another investor.
- Pretend to have used a product when you have not.
- Fabricate trading experience.
- Manufacture testimonials.
- Hide the purpose of research when directly asked.

---

## Rule 3 — Do Not Give Investment Advice

AIIP customer discovery is research.

Do not use outreach to tell someone:

- What asset they should buy.
- What asset they should sell.
- What return they will receive.
- What investment will make them rich.
- That AIIP can guarantee profits.

---

## Rule 4 — Do Not Ask for Money During Initial Outreach

Initial outreach should establish whether a genuine problem exists.

Do not request:

- Payments.
- Deposits.
- Investment capital.
- Wallet transfers.
- Private keys.
- Seed phrases.
- Exchange credentials.

Payment discussions belong later in the validation process.

# Outreach Sequence

## Stage 1 — Public Context

Start from the person's actual public post.

Understand:

- What decision they are making.
- What problem they described.
- Whether they appear to be actively experiencing it.

---

## Stage 2 — Qualification

Determine:

- Are they actually involved in digital assets?
- Are they currently making investment decisions?
- Is the problem recent?
- Is the problem recurring?
- What tools do they currently use?
- Have they already paid for a solution?

---

## Stage 3 — Conversation

Explore the customer's actual behavior.

Questions include:

- What were you trying to decide?
- What triggered the decision?
- What information did you look at?
- Which sources did you trust?
- Did different sources disagree?
- How long did the research take?
- What did you ultimately do?
- What happened afterward?

---

## Stage 4 — Interview

Invite the person to a structured research conversation only after genuine interest or problem evidence appears.

Record the interview using:

`../interviews/interview-guide.md`

---

## Stage 5 — Product Test

Only after the problem is sufficiently understood should an AIIP product test be offered.

The current candidate product is:

**AIIP Decision Intelligence Report**

See:

`../../../content/aiip-research/products/decision-intelligence-report/`

---

## Stage 6 — Commercial Validation

Only after the customer has experienced the product should willingness to pay be evaluated.

Strong evidence includes:

- Existing spending on similar tools.
- Request for continued access.
- Request for additional reports.
- Explicit willingness to pay.
- Actual payment.

# Lead Classification

## Signal

Public post suggests a potential problem.

## Qualified Lead

The person appears to participate in the relevant market and experience a relevant problem.

## Conversation

The person responds and discusses their situation.

## Interview

The person participates in structured discovery.

## Problem Confirmed

The customer describes a genuine problem supported by recent behavior.

## Product Test

The customer evaluates an AIIP product.

## Commercial Signal

The customer demonstrates willingness to pay.

## Customer

The customer actually pays for an AIIP product.

# Evidence Rule

Do not upgrade a lead because of enthusiasm.

For example:

> "Sounds interesting."

is weak evidence.

Whereas:

> "I spent three hours comparing five sources before making this decision, and I still wasn't sure."

is stronger problem evidence.

And:

> "I tested the report, used it for my next decision, and want another report."

is strong product evidence.

Actual payment is the strongest commercial evidence.

# Success Condition

AIIP-012 succeeds if it produces reliable evidence about:

1. Who experiences the problem.
2. What problem they actually experience.
3. How they currently solve it.
4. What existing solutions they use.
5. What existing solutions cost.
6. Whether AIIP improves the workflow.
7. Whether customers will use the product.
8. Whether customers will pay.

The purpose of AIIP-012 is evidence, not activity.
