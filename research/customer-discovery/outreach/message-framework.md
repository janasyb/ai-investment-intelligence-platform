# AIIP Customer Outreach Message Framework

## Purpose

Provide a consistent, respectful framework for contacting people whose public posts indicate a potential AIIP customer problem.

## Core Rule

Lead with curiosity, not the product.

The first interaction should seek to understand the customer's existing behavior and problem.

Do not immediately pitch AIIP.

## Outreach Sequence

### Stage 1 � Contextual Response

Respond directly to the problem the person publicly described.

Example:

> Your point about fragmented crypto research caught my attention. I'm researching how active digital-asset investors make BUY/HOLD/SELL decisions when different sources give conflicting signals. What sources do you normally rely on before making that decision?

### Stage 2 � Conversation

If the person responds, explore:

- What decision were they trying to make?
- What triggered the decision?
- What information did they use?
- Which sources did they trust?
- Which sources conflicted?
- What was difficult?
- What did they ultimately do?
- What happened afterward?

### Stage 3 � Research Disclosure

When appropriate, explain:

> I'm building AIIP, an investment-intelligence platform focused on helping digital-asset investors structure and evaluate investment decisions. I'm currently conducting customer research rather than trying to sell anything.

### Stage 4 � Interview Request

If the conversation indicates a genuine problem:

> I'm trying to understand this problem properly. Would you be open to a short research conversation about how you currently handle it?

### Stage 5 � Product Test

Only after understanding the problem:

> I'm testing an early AIIP decision-intelligence report for a specific digital asset. If you're interested, I can prepare an early version and I'd value your feedback on whether it actually improves your research process.

## What Not To Do

Do not:

- impersonate another investor
- pretend to be an independent customer
- fabricate experience
- promise investment returns
- guarantee profits
- pressure someone to purchase
- spam identical messages
- hide the commercial purpose when directly asked
- request funds during initial discovery
- provide personalized financial advice while pretending to conduct research

## Message Quality Standard

Each outreach message should be:

1. Relevant to the person's actual post.
2. Short enough to read easily.
3. Specific rather than generic.
4. Non-promotional during initial discovery.
5. Focused on learning.
6. Respectful of the person's choice not to respond.

## Success Metric

The goal of outreach is not reply volume.

The goal is:

> meaningful conversations with people experiencing the problem AIIP intends to solve.

## Research Ethics

AIIP customer discovery must be conducted transparently and respectfully.

### Requirements

- Do not impersonate customers, investors, analysts, journalists, or researchers.
- Do not misrepresent AIIP's capabilities.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, customer results, or product usage.
- Do not make guaranteed-return claims.
- Do not pressure people into providing money.
- Do not request private keys, seed phrases, passwords, exchange credentials, or account access.
- Do not scrape or store unnecessary personal information.
- Respect requests not to be contacted.
- Do not repeatedly contact people who do not respond.
- When the conversation becomes product-related, identify AIIP and its commercial nature clearly.
- Keep customer research separate from investment advice.

### Objective

The purpose of customer discovery is to learn whether AIIP solves a real problem.

It is not to manipulate people into becoming customers.
