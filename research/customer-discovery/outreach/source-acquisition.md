# AIIP-012 Lead Source Acquisition

## Purpose

This document defines the process for acquiring publicly verifiable customer-discovery signals for AIIP-012.

The objective is to identify real people publicly discussing genuine digital-asset investment decisions and problems.

A signal must be independently verifiable before it becomes eligible for outreach.

---

## Principle

AIIP customer discovery must be based on evidence.

Do not manufacture leads.

Do not infer identities.

Do not fabricate URLs.

Do not treat search snippets as verified sources.

Do not treat a similar post as the original post.

Do not contact a person until the source has been verified.
