# AIIP-012 Outreach Batch 001

## Purpose

This batch represents the first controlled customer-discovery outreach experiment for AIIP-012.

The purpose is to test whether high-quality digital-asset decision signals can be converted into genuine customer conversations.

This is not a marketing campaign.

---

## Batch Strategy

Initial outreach targets the strongest confirmed digital-asset signals.

### Batch A

1. LEAD-003 — Exchange Risk / Due Diligence
2. LEAD-002 — SELL/HOLD Decision
3. LEAD-001 — Asset Selection

### Batch B

4. LEAD-004 — Information Fragmentation
5. LEAD-005 — Crypto Operations

Batch B should begin after Batch A responses have been reviewed.

# Lead 003

## Problem Signal

The person asked:

> How do you actually vet a smaller exchange before putting real money on it?

## Hypothesis Being Tested

Active crypto investors may struggle to evaluate exchange risk, liquidity, execution quality, and trustworthiness before committing capital.

## Qualification

Status: Pending

Required before outreach:

- Confirm original post
- Confirm public username
- Confirm post URL
- Confirm the person appears to be discussing their own/current behavior

## Initial Message

Use the actual public post as context.

Suggested message:

> Your question about vetting smaller exchanges caught my attention. I'm researching how active crypto investors evaluate exchange risk before putting real money on an exchange. What do you normally check before deciding whether an exchange is trustworthy?

## Objective

Learn:

- What the person currently checks
- Which sources they use
- How long evaluation takes
- What information is difficult to obtain
- Whether they have experienced an exchange-related problem
- Whether they currently pay for any research or risk tools

Do not pitch AIIP during the initial message.

# Lead 002

## Problem Signal

The person asked:

> If a coin drops 90%, should I sell what is left or ride it to zero?

## Hypothesis Being Tested

Digital-asset investors may struggle to make SELL/HOLD decisions when market conditions change dramatically.

## Qualification

Status: Pending

Required before outreach:

- Confirm original post
- Confirm public username
- Confirm post URL
- Confirm current/recent investment context

## Initial Message

Suggested message:

> Your question about whether to sell after a major drop caught my attention. I'm researching how crypto investors make HOLD, SELL, or WAIT decisions when an asset moves sharply against them. What information do you normally look at before making that decision?

## Objective

Learn:

- What triggered the decision
- What information was available
- What information was missing
- Whether multiple sources conflicted
- How much time was spent researching
- What the person ultimately did
- What happened afterward

Do not provide an answer to the investment question.

# Lead 001

> What memecoins should I buy right now?

## Qualification

Status: Pending

# Batch Rules

## Do Not

- Send all three messages simultaneously without recording them.
- Send generic promotional messages.
- Recommend investments.
- Promise returns.
- Ask for money.
- Ask for wallet information.
- Ask for private keys.
- Ask for exchange credentials.
- Claim AIIP has solved the problem.
- Classify silence as rejection.
- Classify enthusiasm as validation.

## Do

- Personalize each message to the actual public post.
- Record every actual outreach attempt.
- Record the date.
- Record the platform.
- Record the message variant.
- Record the response.
- Preserve the customer's actual words.
- Separate facts from interpretation.

---

# Batch Success Criteria

The batch is not successful merely because messages are sent.

Evidence should progress toward:

Signal
→ Qualified Lead
→ Outreach
→ Response
→ Conversation
→ Interview
→ Problem Confirmation
→ Product Test

Commercial validation remains separate.
