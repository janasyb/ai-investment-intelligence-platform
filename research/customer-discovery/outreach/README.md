# AIIP Customer Outreach

## Purpose

This directory manages AIIP's early customer-discovery outreach process.

The objective is to convert qualified customer signals into genuine conversations with potential AIIP customers.

## Operating Principle

A social-media post is a customer signal, not a customer.

AIIP progresses through:

Signal -> Qualification -> Outreach -> Conversation -> Interview -> Product Test -> Payment

## Scope

This process is for customer discovery and validation, not mass marketing or automated solicitation.

## Target Customer

Priority is given to people demonstrating active digital-asset decision-making behavior, including:

- BUY decisions
- HOLD decisions
- SELL decisions
- WAIT decisions
- Asset evaluation
- Risk evaluation
- Exchange due diligence
- Portfolio allocation
- Research and information-synthesis problems

## Related Files

- `outreach-log.csv` — records outreach activity
- `message-framework.md` — approved outreach approaches
- `../customer-signals.csv` — source customer signals
- `../interviews/interview-guide.md` — customer interview process

## Validation Objective

AIIP-012 aims to produce:

- 20+ qualified customer signals
- 10+ customer conversations
- 5+ recurring problem confirmations
- 3+ product tests
- 1+ paying customer

These are validation targets, not assumptions that the targets will be achieved.
