# AIIP Customer Interview Guide

## Objective

Understand how active digital-asset investors make investment decisions, where their current process fails, and whether AIIP can provide meaningful decision intelligence.

## Interview Principle

Do not lead the customer toward an answer.

Ask about real behavior and recent experiences.

Avoid questions that assume the problem exists.

---

## 1. Background

1. How long have you been investing or trading digital assets?
2. What types of digital assets do you normally evaluate?
3. How frequently do you make investment decisions?

---

## 2. Recent Decision

Ask:

> Walk me through the most recent time you had to decide whether to buy, hold, sell, or wait on a digital asset.

Follow up:

- Which asset?
- What triggered the decision?
- What were you considering?
- How much time did you spend researching?
- What did you ultimately decide?

---

## 3. Information Sources

Ask:

> What information did you look at before making that decision?

Explore:

- Market data
- Price history
- Fundamental analysis
- Tokenomics
- On-chain data
- Exchange and liquidity information
- Social media
- News
- Research reports
- Influencers
- AI tools
- Trading indicators
- Portfolio tools

Then ask:

> Which source did you trust most, and why?

---

## 4. Conflicting Information

Ask:

> Did you encounter information that disagreed with other sources?

If yes:

- What conflicted?
- How did you decide which source to trust?
- How long did resolving the conflict take?
- Did uncertainty affect your decision?

---

## 5. Current Workflow

Ask:

> If you had to repeat that research process today, what would you do first?

Document:

- Tools used
- Websites used
- Data sources
- Manual steps
- Time required
- Repeated work
- Pain points

---

## 6. Failure Points

Ask:

> What is the weakest part of your current research process?

Then:

> What information do you wish you had before making the decision?

---

## 7. Consequences

Ask:

> What happens when you make the wrong decision?

Explore:

- Financial impact
- Opportunity cost
- Time
- Stress
- Loss of confidence
- Portfolio impact

Do not ask customers to disclose private financial credentials or account information.

---

## 8. Existing Spending

Ask:

> Do you currently pay for any research, analytics, signals, portfolio tools, data, or investment information?

If yes:

- What?
- How much approximately?
- Why do you pay for it?
- What value does it provide?
- What does it fail to provide?

Existing spending is stronger evidence than hypothetical willingness to pay.

---

## 9. AIIP Concept Test

Only after understanding the existing problem:

> AIIP is an early investment-intelligence platform focused on helping digital-asset investors structure and evaluate investment decisions. We are testing whether a research report that synthesizes market, fundamental, tokenomic, liquidity, risk, and other relevant information would improve the decision process.

Then ask:

> What would you expect this report to tell you?

Do not suggest the answer.

---

## 10. Product Test

If appropriate:

> Would you be willing to evaluate an early AIIP report for a specific asset and tell us what is useful, missing, or misleading?

Record:

- Accepted
- Declined
- Why

---

## 11. Willingness To Pay

Progress from behavior to commitment.

Ask:

> Have you paid for anything similar before?

Then:

> What would make this valuable enough to pay for?

After the customer has tested the product:

> If this report were available today, would you pay for it?

If yes:

> What price range would you consider reasonable?

Record actual statements rather than interpreting them optimistically.

---

## 12. Closing

Ask:

> What is the one thing you would change about your current investment research process?

Then:

> Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?

Thank the participant.

---

# Evidence Classification

## Weak Evidence

Customer expresses general interest.

## Moderate Evidence

Customer describes a recurring problem and existing workaround.

## Strong Evidence

Customer has:

- experienced the problem recently
- spent time or money solving it
- tested an AIIP solution
- changed behavior because of the solution
- or paid for the solution

## Interview Outcome

Each interview should conclude with one primary classification:

- Problem Not Confirmed
- Problem Confirmed
- Existing Solution Sufficient
- AIIP Solution Potentially Valuable
- Product Test Accepted
- Product Test Rejected
- Willing To Pay
- Paid

Do not classify an interview as validated merely because the customer is enthusiastic.
