# AIIP Customer Interviews

## Purpose

Understand how real people make digital-asset investment decisions.

The objective is discovery, not sales.

---

# Interview Protocol

## Question 1

What digital asset are you currently making a decision about?

## Question 2

What decision are you trying to make?

- BUY
- HOLD
- SELL
- WAIT
- Other

## Question 3

What's making that decision difficult?

## Question 4

What information are you currently using?

## Question 5

Where do you get that information?

## Question 6

What's missing, unreliable, or difficult to interpret?

## Question 7

If you could have one piece of analysis before making the decision, what would it be?

## Question 8

How are you solving this problem today?

## Question 9

What happens if you make the wrong decision?

## Question 10

If a service could reliably solve this problem, would you pay for it?

---

# Interview Rules

1. Do not pitch AIIP at the beginning.
2. Do not tell the customer what they should think.
3. Do not lead the customer toward crypto.
4. Record their actual words where useful.
5. Separate facts from assumptions.
6. Look for repeated problems.
7. Do not treat interest as willingness to pay.
8. Treat an actual payment as stronger evidence than verbal interest.
