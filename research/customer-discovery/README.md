# AIIP Customer Discovery

This directory contains the customer-discovery system for AIIP Technologies.

## Objective

Determine:

> Who needs AIIP, what problem they have, and what they will pay to solve.

## Customer Signal Score

| Factor | Points |
|---|---:|
| Clearly owns/trades digital assets | 2 |
| Has an active investment decision | 2 |
| Decision is BUY/HOLD/SELL/WAIT | 2 |
| Expresses uncertainty/problem | 1 |
| Has money/capital at risk | 1 |
| Currently searching for information | 1 |
| Existing solutions are insufficient | 1 |

Maximum: 10

## Priority

0–3: Ignore for now

4–5: Interesting

6–7: Potential customer

8–9: High-priority prospect

10: Exceptional discovery lead

## Important

A customer signal is not a customer.

A customer is validated through behavior, engagement, product usage, and ultimately willingness to pay.
