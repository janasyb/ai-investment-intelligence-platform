# AIIP Customer Discovery

This directory contains the customer-discovery system for AIIP Technologies.

Determine:

> Who needs AIIP, what problem they have, and what they will pay to solve.

## Customer Signal Score

| Factor | Points |
|---|---:|
| Clearly owns/trades digital assets | 2 |
| Has an active investment decision | 2 |
| Decision is BUY/HOLD/SELL/WAIT | 2 |
| Expresses uncertainty/problem | 1 |
| Has money/capital at risk | 1 |
| Currently searching for information | 1 |
| Existing solutions are insufficient | 1 |

Maximum: 10

## Priority

0–3: Ignore for now

4–5: Interesting

6–7: Potential customer

8–9: High-priority prospect

10: Exceptional discovery lead

## Important

A customer signal is not a customer.

A customer is validated through behavior, engagement, product usage, and ultimately willingness to pay.

## Signal Freshness and AIIP-018 Eligibility

Customer signals are historical evidence until the underlying situation is confirmed as current.

### Freshness

- `FRESH` - signal is 0-14 days old
- `STALE` - signal is more than 14 days old without current confirmation
- `REVALIDATED` - an older signal has been contacted and the person confirms that the underlying decision is still current

Freshness is determined from the signal date or the date of successful revalidation.

### AIIP-018 Eligibility

A prospect is eligible for the current AIIP-018 commercial-validation experiment only when:

1. the person has a current or sufficiently recent digital-asset investment decision
2. the decision can be confirmed through current interaction
3. the customer problem remains relevant
4. the prospect is willing to participate in the research workflow

A stale signal is not an AIIP-018 validation candidate unless the underlying decision is revalidated.

### Outreach Timing

For a fresh signal:

- Initial outreach: Day 0
- One respectful follow-up: approximately Day 3-5
- Close as no response: approximately Day 7-10

Do not repeatedly contact a non-responsive prospect.

### Historical Leads

Stale leads must not be deleted merely because they are no longer current.

Historical leads remain useful for:

- customer-discovery evidence
- hypothesis development
- pattern analysis
- measuring what has already been attempted

Historical evidence must not be presented as current customer demand.

### Core Rule

> A signal is evidence of a possible problem. A current, responsive, qualified prospect is an AIIP-018 validation candidate.
