# AI Investment Intelligence Platform (AIIP)

> Discover. Analyze. Invest. Grow.

## Overview

The AI Investment Intelligence Platform (AIIP) is an AI-powered platform for discovering, evaluating, and managing investments in digital assets.

The platform combines artificial intelligence, market intelligence, data analytics, and investment research to help investors identify high-value opportunities before the broader market.

The first supported asset class is premium domain names, with future support planned for websites, SaaS businesses, AI assets, APIs, datasets, digital products, and other transferable digital assets.
